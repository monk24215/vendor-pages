What Happens When You Put Raw Beef

Over A Dead Fire

   You’ll also find out why our ancestors would often hang beef

   rather thinly and place it over sticks or a wooden pyramid.

   whole years without refrigeration. So, if you ever find
   yourself in a blackout or stranded without access to
   electricity just remember that all you really need to preserve
