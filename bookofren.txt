   Marshmallow – The Most Powerful Plant for a Healthy Digestive
   System

   When most people hear marshmallow, they tend to picture the
   white fluffy treat commonly roasted over a campfire.

   most importantly, they contain a sap-like substance called
   mucilage.

   As the marshmallow mucilage goes down through your digestive
   tract, it will coat your stomach, intestines, and colon with an
   additional protective layer, soothing inflammation it finds
   along the way. That’s why this tea also helps people with
   stomach ulcers and digestive disorders, such as heartburn,

   disease.

   the mucilage extract for a few hours. Drink for a soothing and
   restorative effect all along your digestive tract, from mouth
   to colon.
