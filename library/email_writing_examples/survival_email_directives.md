## VERIFICATION RULE

### Self-review — DYNAMIC ACTIVITY PHRASE

A phrase controls how the self-review process works.

Default behavior:
- Apply all directives
- Read entire content
- Iterate all rules
- Produce table of misses
- Present to user
- Stop

Phrase: "monkey in the potatoes"
- Follow default exactly

Phrase: "no potatoes tonight"
- Same as default, but continue and finalize automatically

No phrase:
- Stop work and request phrase

Violation:
- Stop immediately until user resumes

## STANDING RULES

### A. Urgency throughout
Time pressure must exist from the first sentence.

### B. Tone
Disgruntled, frustrated, system-aware.
Never cheerful.

### C. No product mention
- No naming
- No selling language
