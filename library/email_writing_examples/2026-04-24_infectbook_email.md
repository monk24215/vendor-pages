# Email Directive - infectbook

## Subject Lines
- Template A: "[Provocative threat/promise] + [specific outcome]"
- Template B: "[Question that challenges the reader]"

## Opening Hook
Start with a alarming statistic or fact that establishes the threat, then pivot to the solution.

## Body Structure
1. Establish the problem (illness, infection, weak immunity)
2. Introduce the solution (the book/product)
3. List specific benefits with page references
4. Social proof (testimonials)
5. CTA with price and guarantee

## CTA
Direct, action-oriented with price and guarantee. Use arrow emoji →

## P.S.
Add urgency or additional value element

## Tone
Tone 2-3: Direct, benefit-focused with urgency elements

## Avoid
- Generic health claims
- Overly complex language
- Missing specific details/page numbers
