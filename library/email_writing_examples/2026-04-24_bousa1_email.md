## Body Structure
1. Establish the spiritual threat (AI, the beast system, end times)
2. Explain why the "world" doesn't see it
3. Introduce the solution (the book/product)
4. List specific benefits with page references
5. CTA with price and guarantee

## CTA
Direct, action-oriented with price and guarantee. Use arrow emoji →

## P.S.
Add urgency or spiritual encouragement

## Tone
Tone 2-3: Angry, upset, peer-to-peer. The reader is fighting against "the system," "the beast," "the man." Write like you're venting to a brother in Christ who's been wronged.

## Language
7th grade level or below. Short sentences. Simple words.

## Avoid
- Complex theological terms
- Anything above 7th grade reading level
- Soft or apologetic tone
