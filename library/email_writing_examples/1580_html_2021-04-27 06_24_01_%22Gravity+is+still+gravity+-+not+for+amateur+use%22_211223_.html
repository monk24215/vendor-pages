Years ago, I remember reading in the old "Edge Company" knife catalog about some ninja hand and feet claws.

"Gravity is still gravity," read a little disclaimer with the ad. "Not for amateur use."

I always think of that ad when I see people talking about grappling hooks.

I think a lot of them picture using grappling hooks for stuff like this:
While you're not going to be throwing any bat-grapples on buildings and climbing skyscrapers, you may not realize just how useful a MODERN "grappling hook" can be.

- It can help you climb steep hills if you can find an anchor point.

- It can be used as a 'retrieval tool' for areas you can't reach

- It can be swung as a WEAPON, in much the same way thugs use padlocks on the end of ropes or bandannas

- It can become an all-purpose survival hook and connector, for whenever you need to sling your bag over a tree branch, lift something heavy using physics, or anything ELSE.

A modern grappling hook like this one, in other words, is pretty much an all-purpose survival and self-defense tool.

Even better, nobody really thinks of it as that... which means it's covert, too!

No, a grappling hook won't make you Batman (or even Robin)... but it WILL make you more prepared, and that's the whole point!

Get ready. Fight back. Stay alive.
