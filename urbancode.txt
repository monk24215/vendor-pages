   Psychological Resilience: The guide incorporates mental

   urban chaos, including mass panic and the collapse of social
   order, informs the survival strategies. This includes

   potentially dangerous behaviors of others.
   Urban Infrastructure Vulnerabilities: The survival code is
   based on knowledge of critical urban infrastructure systems —
   like electricity, water supply, and communication networks —
   and how they fail during a crisis. Understanding these
   vulnerabilities helps users prepare for and adapt to the
   breakdown of these systems.
   Self-Sufficiency in Urban Settings: The survival strategies are
   grounded in principles of self-sufficiency, such as securing
   water, food, and shelter in urban environments. These methods
   are designed to be practical and achievable even in small
   living spaces, with the focus on readily available resources.
   Behavioral Economics: The code emphasizes low-cost, high-impact
   survival methods based on economic principles, allowing
   families to prepare without the need for expensive survival
   gear. It uses the concept of resourcefulness, focusing on what
   can be done with items already available at home or found
   easily in local stores.
   Risk Assessment and Threat Detection: The guide applies risk
   assessment techniques to help individuals identify danger zones
   in urban environments and develop escape routes, drawing on
   principles from urban planning and safety protocols used in

   Emergency Planning and Logistics: The system incorporates

   communication, based on established principles of emergency
