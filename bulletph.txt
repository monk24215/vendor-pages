Lock all gates and consider installing access control systems, like keypads or smart locks, for enhanced security.
Doors and windows:
Install strong, solid core or metal doors and high-quality deadbolt locks.
Reinforce door strike plates with longer screws (3 inches) to improve kick-in resistance.
Secure windows with locks, safety films that prevent shattering, or even bars or grills for added protection.

What other ways can landscaping deter intruders?
In addition to using lighting and creating clear sightlines, landscaping can deter intruders in several other ways:
1. Creating physical barriers
Thorny plants and hedges: Planting thorny shrubs and hedges, such as roses, holly, barberry, or firethorn, strategically around your property can create a natural barrier that discourages intruders.
Dense plantings: Creating thick hedges or dense planting beds can make it difficult for someone to navigate through and potentially provide a natural deterrent.
Climbing plants: Train thorny climbing plants like bougainvillea over fences and walls to make it harder to scale them. 
2. Eliminating hiding spots
Trim hedges and shrubs: Regularly prune vegetation, especially near windows and doors, to prevent hiding places for intruders.
Tree trimming: Trim tree branches that could provide access to second-story windows or your roof. 
3. Increasing noise
Gravel pathways: Placing gravel or loose stones beneath windows or along pathways can create noise when walked on, alerting you to someone's presence. 
4. Influencing perception
Well-maintained yard: A tidy and cared-for yard can signal that a home is occupied and cared for, making it a less appealing target for burglars.
Open and visible spaces: Creating an open and visible space around your home can deter intruders by eliminating the element of surprise.
Defensive planting: Incorporate visually unappealing plants, such as cacti, succulents, or plants with sharp foliage, that make the property less inviting to intruders. 
By combining these landscaping techniques with other security measures like lighting and surveillance, you can create a comprehensive defense system that maximizes your home's safety and security.
